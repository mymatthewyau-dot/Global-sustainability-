Recommendations
🦪
Add ~800 more Oysters
Filter feeder ratio is too low (2:1 fish:oyster). Adding oysters will absorb ammonia and reduce TSS naturally. Target ratio 5:1 fish:filter feeder.
High priority
🌿
Introduce Seaweed (e.g. Gracilaria)
Seaweed absorbs dissolved inorganic nitrogen — directly addresses elevated ammonia from salmon. IMTA-standard addition for high fish density farms.
High priority
🍽️
Switch to Low-Protein Feed for Tilapia
Reduce feed protein content from ~32% to ~28%. Lower protein → less nitrogen excreted → lower ammonia load. No composition change needed.
Medium priority
📉
Reduce Salmon by ~400 individuals
Salmon have the highest oxygen demand per individual. Trimming to 1,600 would improve DO and reduce ammonia while maintaining profitability.
